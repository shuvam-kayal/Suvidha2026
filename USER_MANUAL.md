# SUVIDHA Kiosk - User Manual

Welcome to the SUVIDHA (Smart Urban Versatile Interface for Daily Help and Assistance) Kiosk! This guide will help you use all the services available.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Touch the screen** to wake up the kiosk
2. **Login with OTP** - Enter your mobile number and verify with the code sent to your phone
3. **Select a service** - Choose from Electricity, Water, Gas, or Municipal services

---

## How to Pay a Bill

### Step 1: Login
- Tap "Login" on the dashboard
- Enter your 10-digit mobile number
- Enter the OTP received on your phone

### Step 2: Select Service
- Tap on the utility type (Electricity, Water, Gas, or Municipal)
- The selected service will be highlighted with a green checkmark

### Step 3: View Bills
- Tap "Go to [Service Name]" button
- Tap "Pay Bills" to see pending bills
- Select a bill to view details

### Step 4: Make Payment
- Tap "Pay Now" button
- Choose payment method (UPI, Card, or Net Banking)
- For UPI: Scan the QR code with any UPI app
- For Card: Insert or tap your card on the reader
- Wait for payment confirmation

### Step 5: Download Receipt
- After payment, tap "Download Receipt"
- The receipt will be saved as PDF
- You can also print the receipt using the kiosk printer

## How to File a Grievance

### Step 1: Navigate to Complaints
- From the dashboard, select your utility service
- Tap "File Complaint" button

### Step 2: Select Category
- Choose the complaint category:
  - **Billing Issues** - Wrong charges, duplicate bills
  - **Supply Problems** - Outages, low pressure
  - **Meter Issues** - Faulty meter, meter reading
  - **Service Quality** - Response delays

### Step 3: Provide Details
- Enter a clear subject line
- Describe the issue in detail
- Add your address and contact if needed

### Step 4: Submit
- Review your complaint
- Tap "Submit Complaint"
- Note your ticket number for tracking

---

## How to Request a New Connection

### Step 1: Access Service Request
- Login to the kiosk
- Tap "New Connection" button on dashboard

### Step 2: Select Request Type
- **New Connection** - For new utility meters
- **Address Change** - To update service address
- **Bulk Waste Pickup** - Schedule waste collection

### Step 3: Fill Application Form
- Enter personal details (Name, Phone, Email)
- Provide address information
- For electricity: Select load requirement (1-5 kW, 5-10 kW, etc.)
- Choose property type (Residential/Commercial)

### Step 4: Review and Submit
- Verify all details on the review screen
- Tap "Submit Request"
- Save your request number for tracking

---

## How to Track Your Complaint

### Step 1: Access Tracker
- Tap "Track Grievance" on the dashboard
- No login required for tracking

### Step 2: Enter Ticket Number
- Enter your complaint ticket number (e.g., GRV-2026-001234)
- Tap "Track" button

### Step 3: View Status
- See current status: Open → In Progress → Resolved
- View timeline of updates
- Check assigned officer details

---

## Emergency Alerts

The scrolling ticker at the bottom shows important information:
- ⚠️ **Yellow** - Weather warnings, maintenance schedules
- ℹ️ **Blue** - Service announcements, deadline extensions
- ✅ **Green** - New features, improvements
- 🔴 **Red** - Emergency alerts, outage notifications

---

## Accessibility Features

- **Voice Assistance** - Tap the speaker icon for audio guidance
- **Language Selection** - Choose English or Hindi from top-right menu
- **Large Touch Targets** - All buttons are sized for easy tapping
- **High Contrast** - Dark theme reduces eye strain
